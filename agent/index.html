STEP-BY-STEP ONBOARDING

Device Setup & Activation Guide

Follow these 5 simple steps to configure your Android smartphone for 100% reliable 24/7 OmniChannel AI automation across all platforms.

⚠️
Important Compatibility Note: OmniChannel AI Agent PRO runs directly on Android Smartphones (Android 8.0 to Android 15). It is not compatible with Apple iPhones (iOS) due to Apple system background notification restrictions.
STEP 01

Download & Install Android APK

Download app-release.apk to your Android phone and install it. If prompted with "Install from Unknown Sources", tap Allow.

🔒 Android 13, 14 & 15 "Restricted Settings" Unlock:

If your phone shows "Restricted Setting — For your security, this setting is currently unavailable" when enabling Accessibility:

  1. Go to Phone Settings ➔ Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent.
  2. Tap the 3 Dots (⋮) in the top right corner.
  3. Select "Allow Restricted Settings".
  4. Enter your Phone PIN or Fingerprint to confirm unlock.
STEP 02

Grant System Permissions

Open the App and grant the required system permissions when prompted:

  • ✅ Notification Listener Permission: Allows the AI Agent to read and auto-reply to incoming customer messages across WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, TikTok & SMS.
  • ✅ Accessibility Service Permission: Enables the AI Agent to auto-attach product catalog photos inside WhatsApp.
  • ✅ Battery Optimization Exemption: Keeps the AI engine running 24/7 in the background without being closed by the system.
STEP 03

Phone Brand Battery Optimization (24/7 Keep-Alive)

To prevent your phone manufacturer from killing the AI background service, select your phone brand below and apply the 1-minute setting:

Xiaomi MIUI / HyperOS Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Manage Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent.
  2. Turn ON "Autostart".
  3. Tap Battery Saver ➔ Choose "No Restrictions".
  4. Open Recent Apps window, long-press WhatsApp AI Agent, and tap the Lock (🔒) icon so it stays pinned.

Samsung OneUI Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent.
  2. Tap Battery ➔ Choose "Unrestricted".
  3. Go to Settings ➔ Device Care ➔ Battery ➔ Background Usage Limits.
  4. Ensure WhatsApp AI Agent is NOT in "Sleeping Apps" or "Deep Sleeping Apps".

Vivo / iQOO FuntouchOS Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Battery ➔ High Background Power Consumption.
  2. Find WhatsApp AI Agent and turn the toggle ON.
  3. Go to Settings ➔ Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent ➔ Autostart and turn ON.

Oppo / Realme ColorOS Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Battery ➔ App Battery Management.
  2. Select WhatsApp AI Agent ➔ Turn ON "Allow Background Activity" & "Allow Auto Launch".
STEP 04

Configure WhatsApp & Social Media Apps

Make sure notifications are turned on for your business messaging apps:

  • 💬 Turn App Notifications ON: Ensure notifications are active for WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, and TikTok in Phone Settings.
  • 📥 WhatsApp Media Auto-Download: Go to WhatsApp Settings ➔ Storage & Data ➔ Media Auto-Download and check Photos (When connected to Mobile Data & Wi-Fi) so Vision AI can process incoming receipt photos.
  • 🔔 Unmute Business Chats: Ensure customer chats or Facebook Page notifications are not muted so incoming alerts reach the AI Agent.
STEP 05

Enter License Key & Start AI Agent

Open the OmniChannel AI Agent App, go to the License tab, paste your License Key (e.g. URBAN-PRO-XXXX-XXXX), select your active channels in Settings, and tap Activate & Start Agent.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Can the AI Agent manage WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram at the same time? ▼
Yes! The OmniChannel AI Agent runs 24/7 on your Android smartphone and listens simultaneously to incoming customer messages across WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct, Telegram, TikTok, and SMS. You can toggle each platform ON or OFF independently anytime in the app Settings.
Can I choose to run only WhatsApp and turn off other social media apps? ▼
Absolutely! In the Settings screen under "Active Messaging Channels", each channel (WhatsApp Business, WhatsApp Personal, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, TikTok, LinkedIn, SMS) has its own independent toggle switch. You can run WhatsApp only, Socials only, or all 7 platforms together.
Does my Android phone need to stay turned on for the bot to reply? ▼
Yes, the OmniChannel AI Agent runs directly as a background foreground service on your Android smartphone. As long as your phone has an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or Mobile Data) and battery optimization is disabled, the AI responds 24/7.
Does the bot support Voice Messages from customers? ▼
Yes! When customers send voice notes on WhatsApp, the built-in speech engine transcribes and processes their audio message using Gemini Multimodal AI, providing an instant polite Sinhala response with product details and prices.
Will my WhatsApp or social media accounts get banned for using this? ▼
No! Unlike unauthorized web-scrapers or third-party bots that reverse-engineer protocols, our agent uses standard Android Notifications and Accessibility APIs to reply just like a human typing on the device, with built-in Anti-Bot loop breakers, reply cooldowns, and human takeover detection.
Can I customize the greeting message and store details? ▼
Yes! In the Settings screen under General Settings, you can enable and type any custom Customer Welcome Message with {store_name} tag support, choose from pre-made business presets (Tuition, Boutique, Studio, General Store), and view a real-time live preview.
How does the Fake Bank Slip Detection work? ▼
When a customer sends a bank receipt image across WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, or Instagram Direct, our Multimodal Vision AI inspects the text, fonts, pixel alignment, and transaction reference against known bank formats (BOC, Commercial, Sampath, HNB, etc.) to ensure the slip hasn't been edited or reused.
