STEP-BY-STEP ONBOARDING

Device Setup & Activation Guide

Follow these 5 simple steps to configure your Android smartphone for 100% reliable 24/7 WhatsApp AI automation.

⚠️
Important Compatibility Note: WhatsApp AI Agent PRO runs exclusively on Android Smartphones (Android 8.0 to Android 15). It is not compatible with Apple iPhones (iOS) due to Apple system background restrictions.
STEP 01

Download & Install Android APK

Download app-debug.apk to your Android phone and install it. If prompted with "Install from Unknown Sources", tap Allow.

🔒 Android 13, 14 & 15 "Restricted Settings" Unlock:

If your phone shows "Restricted Setting — For your security, this setting is currently unavailable" when enabling Accessibility:

  1. Go to Phone Settings ➔ Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent.
  2. Tap the 3 Dots (⋮) in the top right corner.
  3. Select "Allow Restricted Settings".
  4. Enter your Phone PIN or Fingerprint to confirm unlock.
STEP 02

Grant System Permissions

Open the App and grant the required system permissions when prompted:

  • ✅ Notification Listener Permission: Allows the AI Agent to read incoming customer WhatsApp messages.
  • ✅ Accessibility Service Permission: Enables the AI Agent to auto-attach product catalog photos inside WhatsApp.
  • ✅ Battery Optimization Exemption: Keeps the AI engine running 24/7 in the background without being closed.
STEP 03

Phone Brand Battery Optimization (24/7 Keep-Alive)

To prevent your phone manufacturer from killing the AI background service, select your phone brand below and apply the 1-minute setting:

Xiaomi MIUI / HyperOS Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Manage Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent.
  2. Turn ON "Autostart".
  3. Tap Battery Saver ➔ Choose "No Restrictions".
  4. Open Recent Apps window, long-press WhatsApp AI Agent, and tap the Lock (🔒) icon so it stays pinned.

Samsung OneUI Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent.
  2. Tap Battery ➔ Choose "Unrestricted".
  3. Go to Settings ➔ Device Care ➔ Battery ➔ Background Usage Limits.
  4. Ensure WhatsApp AI Agent is NOT in "Sleeping Apps" or "Deep Sleeping Apps".

Vivo / iQOO FuntouchOS Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Battery ➔ High Background Power Consumption.
  2. Find WhatsApp AI Agent and turn the toggle ON.
  3. Go to Settings ➔ Apps ➔ WhatsApp AI Agent ➔ Autostart and turn ON.

Oppo / Realme ColorOS Optimization Guide:

  1. Open Settings ➔ Battery ➔ App Battery Management.
  2. Select WhatsApp AI Agent ➔ Turn ON "Allow Background Activity" & "Allow Auto Launch".
STEP 04

Configure WhatsApp Settings

Make sure your WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business App settings are configured properly:

  • 💬 Turn Notifications ON: Ensure WhatsApp Notifications are active in Phone Settings & WhatsApp App Settings.
  • 📥 Media Auto-Download: Go to WhatsApp Settings ➔ Storage & Data ➔ Media Auto-Download and check Photos (When connected to Mobile Data & Wi-Fi) so Vision AI can process incoming receipt photos.
  • 🔔 Unmute Business Chats: Ensure customer chats are not muted so notifications are received.
STEP 05

Enter License Key & Start AI Agent

Open the WhatsApp AI Agent App, go to the License tab, paste your License Key (e.g. URBAN-PRO-XXXX-XXXX), and tap Activate & Start Agent.

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Does my Android phone need to stay turned on for the bot to reply? ▼
Yes, the WhatsApp AI Agent runs directly as a background foreground service on your Android smartphone. As long as your phone has an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or Mobile Data) and battery optimization is disabled, the AI responds 24/7.
Will my WhatsApp account get banned for using this? ▼
No! Unlike unauthorized web-scrapers or third-party WhatsApp bots that reverse-engineer protocols, our agent uses standard Android Notifications and Accessibility APIs to reply just like a human typing on the device, with built-in Anti-Bot loop breakers and reply cooldowns.
Can I customize the greeting message and store details? ▼
Yes! In the Settings screen under General Settings, you can enable and type any custom Customer Welcome Message with {store_name} tag support, choose from pre-made business presets (Tuition, Boutique, Studio, General Store), and view a real-time live preview.
How does the Fake Bank Slip Detection work? ▼
When a customer sends a bank receipt image, our Multimodal Vision AI inspects the text, fonts, pixel alignment, and transaction reference against known bank formats (BOC, Commercial, Sampath, HNB, etc.) to ensure the slip hasn't been edited or reused.
